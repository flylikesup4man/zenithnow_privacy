Privacy Policy for Zenith Now

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zenith Now ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Zenith Now mobile application.

By using Zenith Now, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Zenith Now may collect or process the following types of information:

Google Account Information

Zenith Now allows users to sign in using their Google account. When you sign in with Google, we may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google account profile picture
  • A unique account identifier

Zenith Now does not receive or store your Google account password. Authentication is handled directly through Google's authentication services.

Location Information

Zenith Now may request access to your device's location in order to provide satellite tracking features, including displaying satellites near your location and calculating satellite visibility and passes.

Location information is used only for features that require knowledge of your approximate or precise position.

Depending on the features you use, location information may be processed on your device or transmitted to services required to calculate or retrieve satellite-related information.

You can disable location access at any time through your device settings. However, some features of Zenith Now may not work correctly without location permission.

Device and Usage Information

The app or third-party services integrated into the app may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Basic app usage information

This information may be used to improve application stability, performance, and user experience.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through Zenith Now may be used to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain user accounts
  • Provide satellite tracking and visibility features
  • Calculate satellite positions relative to the user's location
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and technical problems
  • Prevent abuse or unauthorized activity

3. Google Sign-In

Zenith Now uses Google Sign-In for authentication.

Google's collection and use of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Zenith Now may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect certain information about your device, advertising identifiers, interactions with advertisements, and other technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Zenith Now may use third-party services to provide authentication, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, satellite data, or other application functionality.

Third-party services used by the app may include:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Services

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with third-party service providers only when necessary to provide application functionality, comply with legal obligations, protect users, or maintain the security of the application.
